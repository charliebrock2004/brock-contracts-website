About us

Joiners and builders based in Crieff

A joinery and building contracting business based at Holly Place in Crieff, working across Strathearn and Perthshire for over 15 years.

We take on joinery and general building work across Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area, from a set of internal doors to a new house.

Who we are

Brock Contracts is a joinery and building contracting business based in Crieff. We work as principal contractor or sub-contractor depending on what a job needs, managing every trade on site so the work runs to programme and finishes properly.

How we work

Most of the work we take on is local. Being based in Crieff means we know the housing stock, the suppliers and the building warrant process with Perth & Kinross Council.

  • Joinery and carpentry, including bespoke fitted work and staircases
  • New builds, extensions and renovations
  • Kitchens, doors and windows, supplied and fitted
  • Flooring and roofing

Where we work

Our base is 3 Holly Place, Crieff, PH7 3EP. We work across Crieff, Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area. See our recent projects for where we’ve been lately.
