About Us

Joiners and builders in Crieff

Joiners and building contractors based at Holly Place, working across Strathearn and Perthshire for over 15 years.

Who we are

Brock Contracts is a joinery and building contracting business based in Crieff. We take on joinery and general building work across Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area.

We work as principal contractor or sub-contractor depending on what a job needs, managing every trade on site so the work runs to programme and finishes properly.

How we work

Most of the work we take on is local. Being based in Crieff means we know the housing stock, the suppliers and the building warrant process with Perth & Kinross Council.

  • Joinery and carpentry, including bespoke fitted work
  • New builds, extensions and full renovations
  • Kitchen fitting, coordinated with the other trades
  • Roofing, flooring, doors and windows
  • Project management as principal or sub-contractor

Where we work

Our base is 3 Holly Place, Crieff, PH7 3EP. We work across Crieff, Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area — see recent projects.
