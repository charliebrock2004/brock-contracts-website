About Us

A family firm in Crieff

Third-generation joiners and building contractors, based at Holly Place and working across Strathearn and Perthshire.

Three generations of joiners and builders

Brock Contracts is a third-generation, family-run joinery and building contracting business based in Crieff. We take on joinery and general building work across Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area.

We work as principal contractor or sub-contractor depending on what a job needs, managing every trade on site so the work runs to programme and finishes properly.

How we work

Most of the work we take on is local. Being based in Crieff means we know the housing stock, the suppliers and the building warrant process with Perth & Kinross Council.

  • Joinery and carpentry, including bespoke fitted work
  • New builds, extensions and full renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom fitting coordinated with other trades
  • Project management as principal or sub-contractor
