About Us

Joiners and builders, based in Crieff.

Brock Contracts is a joinery and building contracting business based in Crieff. We take on joinery and general building work across Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area, and have done for over fifteen years.

We work as principal contractor or sub-contractor depending on what a job needs, managing every trade on site so the work runs to programme and finishes properly.

  • Joiners and building contractors, over 15 years in business
  • Every trade coordinated under one contract
  • Based in Crieff, working throughout Perthshire
