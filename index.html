About Us

A family firm, three generations on.

Brock Contracts is a third-generation, family-run contracting business based in Crieff. We take on joinery and general building work across Strathearn and the wider Perthshire area.

We work as principal contractor or sub-contractor depending on what a job needs, managing every trade on site so the work runs to programme and finishes properly.
