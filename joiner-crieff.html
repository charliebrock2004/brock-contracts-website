Joiners in Crieff

Bespoke joinery, fitted units, doors and staircases from a joinery and building firm based in Crieff.

Brock Contracts is a firm of joiners based at Holly Place in Crieff. We take on everything from a set of internal doors to bespoke fitted joinery, staircases and kitchen installations — and because we also work as a building contractor, we can handle the structural side of a job as well as the finish.

Our joinery services

  • Bespoke fitted joinery — units, boot rooms, shelving and cabinetry
  • Doors and staircases
  • Kitchens and fitted units
  • General carpentry and repairs — no minimum job size
